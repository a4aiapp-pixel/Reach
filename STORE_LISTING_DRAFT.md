## Full description
Reach helps adults discover meaningful short-term offers and requests. Offer a skill, request help, or connect with a trusted group. Find relevant people in your city, across your country, worldwide, or within a broad nearby area you explicitly choose to share.

Create a Reach in seconds: what you can offer or need, its category and how long you're available. Others can introduce themselves through a request. Accept before private chat opens. Keep your identity and control private with blocking, reporting, and optional nearby discovery.

Key features: time-limited offers and requests, city/country/approximate-nearby discovery, invite-only circles, mutual-consent connections, real-time text conversations, custom profile, in-app activity alerts, blocking and reporting, and account deletion.

Reach is for people aged 18+. Stated skills and qualifications are self-declared, not independently verified. Location is approximate and optional. Availability is never a guarantee that another member can provide a service or that an offline meeting is safe.

**Do not advertise:** background push notifications unless operationally configured and tested; verified identity/credentials; E2EE; paid services; AI matching; live worldwide active community until populated.
