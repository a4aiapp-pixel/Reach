Child Safety Standards

Reach is for adults aged 18 years or older. Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) are absolutely prohibited. Reach explicitly bans sexualizing minors, grooming, sextortion involving minors, trafficking of children, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), recruiting or contacting children for abuse, and sharing or soliciting such content.

Reach's date-of-birth age check is self-attested, not government-ID verification. People discovery and calling are off by default, and new media uploads are restricted until adequate screening exists. These safeguards do not guarantee that minors cannot misrepresent their age or that every prohibited item will be detected.

Report child safety concerns

Use the in-app Report function on a post, public group, account or message (where available), choose the nearest relevant safety category, and block the user. You can also submit an external confidential report using the Reach support form and select Child safety. Do not attach or redistribute suspected CSAM. Include only the minimum text needed to identify the concern. Do not conduct your own investigation or contact a suspected victim through the app.

Handling and escalation

The operator must review reports, remove prohibited content, restrict offending accounts, preserve necessary evidence as permitted by law, and report confirmed CSAM to the relevant authority in accordance with applicable law. For immediate risk to a child, contact local law enforcement or child-protection services directly. Reach's support inbox is not an emergency line and does not guarantee a real-time response.

Privacy and cooperation

Private information about children and suspected victims must not be published. Reports are restricted to the operator. Reach must cooperate with legitimate lawful requests in accordance with applicable privacy and legal obligations. The developer must designate an accountable child-safety point of contact in the Google Play Console before publication.

Reach is a community-help platform for people aged 18 and older. The operator must monitor the support inbox and maintain accurate Google Play developer contact information. Policy updated 19 November 2026.
