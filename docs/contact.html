Contact Reach

Use this form for account help, privacy/deletion difficulties, abuse, scams or child-safety concerns. Your message is visible only to the Reach operator. Do not include passwords, payment details, exact addresses or attachments. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local authorities—this form is not monitored as an emergency service.

Privacy: The form stores your supplied email and message; a network-derived hash is used to limit spam. Do not send illegal imagery.

Reach is a community-help platform for people aged 18 and older. The operator must monitor the support inbox and maintain accurate Google Play developer contact information. Policy updated 18 November 2026.
