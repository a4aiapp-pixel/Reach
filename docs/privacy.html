Reach — Privacy policy

Operator publication required: Replace this draft with the legal operator name, support contact, effective date, retention schedule, data hosting location and any country-specific statutory disclosures BEFORE submitting to Google Play.

Data used

Reach stores your email and authentication details with its authentication provider; your public display name, city, country, optionally entered biography and self-declared skills; time-limited offers/requests; circle memberships; connection requests; messages; abuse reports; and an optional 0.1-degree approximate location grid. Exact GPS is used only transiently on your device for rounding and is not uploaded.

Purpose

To authenticate members, match time-limited availability, connect consenting members, detect misuse, and send selected activity notifications. Your precise address and phone number are not required.

Sharing

Posts marked public can be discovered by signed-in members. Circle-only posts can be seen by circle members. Chat messages are shown to their participants. Access is controlled using database row-level security. Data is processed by the configured Supabase project and, if push notifications are enabled, by Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Your choices

You may disable nearby discovery, remove your approximate location, block users, and delete your account in-app or from the external account deletion page. Account deletion permanently removes linked app data using database cascades, subject to any legally required retention described by the operator.

Security and moderation

Reach supports reporting, blocking, and administrator review of reports. Identity, degrees, skills, and credentials entered by members are NOT independently verified. Do not post payment credentials or private addresses.
