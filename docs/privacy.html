Privacy Policy

Effective: 18 November 2026. This policy describes Reach, a community help and requests application. The publisher's verified legal identity and contact details must be matched to the Google Play developer listing before general release. For questions, use our support contact form.

Information processed

Reach uses your email and authentication credentials through Supabase Auth to register and secure accounts; an optional username and display name; city, country, profile description and skills; offers and requests; accepted conversations, private messages and attachments; invitation circles; blocks and reports; and technical metadata needed to operate and protect the service. Support requests include the contact email, message and an irreversible network-derived hash for spam controls. We do not ask for your phone number or exact home address for ordinary signup.

Nearby discovery is optional. If you opt in, the app obtains location with Android permission and rounds latitude/longitude to a roughly 0.1-degree grid before uploading; this approximate value can disclose a general locality. You can remove it in the app. Media/file permissions are requested only when you invoke the relevant functionality. Do not share IDs, banking details or private addresses in public posts.

How information is used

We use this data to authenticate users, show discovery results, connect members who accept requests, deliver conversations and related notifications, handle support, and investigate spam, scams and safety reports. We do not currently offer commercial payments, advertising, cloud model inference or background calling by default. Free Reach Assist utilities primarily operate on the device, without submitting typed text to an external AI provider.

Who can see it

Depending on your selected visibility, offers/requests may be discoverable by signed-in members or limited to invited circles. Other members may see your chosen public profile details. Private messages and attachments are intended for authorized conversation participants. Reports and support inquiries are not public. Reach relies on Supabase, which hosts backend data and authentication; optional Firebase push functionality is disabled in the default build. Information may be processed outside your country through infrastructure providers.

Security and limitations

We use account authentication, server access controls and private file storage. Messages are not advertised as end-to-end encrypted. Attachments are limited by type and size, and are not represented as comprehensively malware-scanned. No online interaction or third-party member identity can be guaranteed safe. Do not use Reach for emergency response.

Retention and deletion

Account content remains while you use Reach or until it is deleted according to app rules. You can request account and associated data deletion using Delete Account or the in-app Profile action. Private attachments must be cleaned up before account deletion can complete. Some system logs or backups may persist temporarily or where retention is legally required; deletion from backups may take additional time. Abuse reports and security records are handled to prevent misuse and support applicable legal obligations; further retention schedules must be finalized by the operator before public launch. Support requests may be retained for handling and safety review and must be periodically purged by the operator.

Your controls

You may edit profile details, disable approximate nearby location, report and block accounts, and delete your account. Android permissions can be changed in device settings. For help or privacy requests use the contact page. Under-18 users are not permitted to register.

Reach is a community-help platform for people aged 18 and older. The operator must monitor the support inbox and maintain accurate Google Play developer contact information. Policy updated 18 November 2026.
