Community Rules & Terms

Audience: Adults aged 18 and above. Reach supports lawful, voluntary community help, user-created public topic groups and invitation-only private circles. Ready-made group ideas are only templates: they do not imply existing members or official endorsements. Users must obtain consent before messaging or meeting, comply with local law, and respect the other person's decision to decline.

Strictly prohibited

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE), grooming, sexualization of minors, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or facilitating such conduct, online or offline.
  • Fraud, impersonation, spam, phishing, extortion, money laundering or selling counterfeit/illegal items.
  • Threats, discriminatory harassment, bullying, stalking, blackmail, non-consensual intimate content or sharing other people's private information.
  • Soliciting sex, coercion, trafficking, exploitation, dangerous or illegal services.
  • Misrepresenting qualifications, arranging unsafe encounters, or uploading executable malware.

Also prohibited: promotion, solicitation or distribution of pornographic and sexually explicit images, videos, live-camera streams and groups; illicit drugs, trafficking, explosives and dangerous instructions; and encouraging self-harm or abuse. Lawful education, addiction recovery, victim support and harm-prevention discussion are not automatically prohibited merely because sensitive words appear.

Reporting and enforcement

Reach includes tools to report objectionable posts, public groups, messages or accounts and block members. High-confidence text patterns and rate limits may automatically block material; reports and appeals still need human review because automated screening is imperfect. Reported material may be reviewed and, where supported by evidence, removed or accounts suspended. Serious safety matters may be referred to lawful authorities. Reporting tools are not monitored continuously as emergency services; for imminent danger contact local authorities directly. You can also send a report through the contact page.

Safe encounters

Choose public places for initial meetings, verify any claimed credentials independently, avoid sending money or confidential credentials to strangers, and never share security OTPs. Reach does not guarantee availability, user identity, outcomes, professional quality or safety.

Accounts and data

By creating an account you acknowledge the Privacy Policy. You can delete your account using the web deletion page or in-app settings. Reach has no integrated commercial payments. Voice/video calls require an accepted connection, individual opt-in by both adults, and a calling-enabled app build. Calls may be unavailable on some networks.

Questions and appeals

Use the support form to raise a safety concern, correct a moderation decision or make a privacy request. The operator must publish the final legal identity and governing terms before public release.
