Reach community rules

Reach is for people aged 18 or older. By using it you agree to engage respectfully and to use it only for legitimate, voluntary, lawful offers and requests.

Prohibited activities include threats, targeted harassment, discriminatory abuse, impersonation, fraud, scams, spam, illegal services, sexual exploitation, requests involving children, coercive arrangements, and sharing another person's private data. Do not publish personal contact information or exact home addresses. Credentials stated by users are not verified.

You may report a post, message, or account, and block any member. Operators review reports and may remove content, suspend accounts, or refer credible threats to authorities where appropriate.

Meet in public places if an offline interaction is needed; avoid sending money or documents to strangers. Reach does not guarantee safety, qualifications, outcome, or identity.

Before publishing, the app operator must finalize governing law, jurisdiction, operator contact information, and any applicable consumer-protection terms.
